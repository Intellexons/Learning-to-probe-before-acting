## Common mistakes to avoid

1. **Do not read env attributes from `state`.** Anything you see on `self` in the env source (e.g. `self.tilt_x`, `self.a_max`, `self.mu`, `self.dt`, `self.goal_radius`, `self.bound`) is **not** in the state dict. `state['tilt_x']` raises `KeyError` at the first call and your policy is discarded.
2. **Do not clamp the action to `state['a_max']`.** `a_max` is hidden — use a conservative constant (e.g. `2.0`) or skip clamping entirely. The env clips for you anyway.
3. **Do not maintain state between calls** (no `last_error`, `error_integral`, `_prev`, global counters, etc.). Each `policy(state)` call is independent; referencing such a variable is an `UnboundLocalError`. Stick to memoryless feedback — PD is fine, PID is not.
